About Billmat LLC

Last updated: September 2026

Our mission

Billmat LLC publishes independent, solution-focused guides to Progressive Insurance payment methods and billing policy. We started this site because the existing information online split cleanly into two unsatisfying categories: Progressive’s own help pages, which are accurate but written to keep you inside their app rather than answer a specific "what happens if" question, and thin third-party content that repeats the same three sentences about "ways to pay your bill" without real numbers.

Why we created this site

We noticed a consistent gap: readers searching for specifics — exact grace period lengths, real late fee ranges, whether a claims payout can cover a premium — were getting vague generalities instead of answers. Every guide on this site is built around a specific, practical question rather than a generic overview.

How we research

We start with Progressive’s official policy documents, billing FAQs, and publicly available rate and rule filings, then cross-check materially important figures — discount percentages, late fees, grace period ranges — against state insurance department publications where available. Where information legitimately varies by state or policy, we say so explicitly rather than presenting an average as if it were universal.

Our fact-checking methodology

Before publishing, every guide is checked line-by-line against source material, dated, and reviewed for figures that may have changed since a prior update. Pages display a "last updated" date, and we schedule an annual review of every guide even if no major policy change has been reported, to catch quieter updates like fee adjustments.

Sources we use and how we cite them

We rely primarily on official insurer documentation and state regulatory publications. When we reference a specific figure — such as a discount percentage or fee range — we describe the type of source behind it directly in the guide text rather than burying attribution in a footnote you’d never read.

Correction policy

If you find outdated or inaccurate information, we want to know. Use our contact page to flag anything specific — ideally with the page and section — and our editorial team will review and correct it, typically within a few business days for factual errors.

Not affiliated with Progressive

Billmat LLC is an independent publisher. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Progressive Casualty Insurance Company or any of its affiliates. "Progressive" is used descriptively to refer to the insurer whose payment policies we cover. For official account access, billing disputes, or coverage questions, contact Progressive directly at 1-800-776-4737 or through their official website.

Commitment to non-promotional, useful content

We don’t sell insurance, and nothing on this site is intended to influence which insurer or payment method you choose. Our only goal is that if you land on a Billmat LLC page with a specific billing question, you leave with an accurate, specific answer.
