Frequently asked questions

What is the Progressive payment phone number?

You can pay by phone using Progressive’s automated system or a live agent at 1-888-620-0950, available 24/7 for automated payments. Have your policy number and payment method ready.

Can I pay Progressive with a credit card?

Yes. Progressive accepts credit card, debit card, PayPal, and direct bank transfer online, through the mobile app, or by phone. See our full payment methods guide for processing times and any fees.

What happens if I miss a Progressive insurance payment?

You enter a grace period that ranges from 7 to 30 days depending on your state. If you pay before it ends, your coverage continues uninterrupted. If not, your policy can be cancelled and you may owe a reinstatement fee. Full detail is in our grace period guide.

How much is the Progressive AutoPay discount?

AutoPay can save up to 15% depending on your state and policy, and it can stack with other administrative discounts. See the AutoPay section of our payment methods guide for a full breakdown.

Can I pay Progressive without logging in?

Yes — Progressive’s Quick Pay tool lets you pay with just your policy number and ZIP code. We cover the exact steps, security considerations, and when to use it instead of your full account in our quick pay guide.
