Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Billmat LLC ("Billmat," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit github.com/progressiveguide/progressivequickpay (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here and to the Terms of Use below.

Information we collect

We collect limited information necessary to operate the Site and respond to inquiries:

  • Contact form submissions: name, email address, and message content you voluntarily provide.
  • Newsletter signups: email address you voluntarily provide to receive occasional updates.
  • Usage data: standard web analytics such as pages visited, approximate location (city/region level), device type, and referring site, collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies.

We do not collect insurance policy numbers, payment card numbers, or bank account details on this Site, and we have no ability to access or modify your Progressive account.

How we use information

  • To respond to contact form submissions and correction reports
  • To send newsletter updates to subscribers who opt in
  • To understand aggregate site usage and improve content
  • To maintain site security and prevent abuse

Cookie policy and tracking

The Site may use cookies or local storage for basic functionality, such as remembering your dark-mode preference, and for aggregate analytics. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect some interactive features (such as remembered theme preference) but will not prevent you from reading content.

Third-party links disclaimer

The Site links to third-party sites, including Progressive’s official website, for reference and account access. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party sites, and linking to them does not imply endorsement or affiliation. Review the privacy policy of any third-party site before providing personal information there.

Affiliate disclosure

Billmat LLC does not currently participate in affiliate programs related to Progressive Insurance or insurance products generally. If this changes in the future, we will update this section and disclose any material financial relationship clearly on affected pages.

Data protection measures

We use industry-standard security practices, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections, to protect data submitted through this Site. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect the limited information we collect.

Email list privacy

If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email address is used solely to send you updates about Progressive payment policy changes and new guides. We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.

GDPR and privacy rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area or another jurisdiction with similar data protection laws, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, and to withdraw consent for communications at any time. Contact us using the details below to exercise these rights.

Content on this Site is provided for general informational purposes only and does not constitute insurance, legal, or financial advice. While we fact-check our guides against publicly available and official sources, payment policies, discounts, and fees can change, and you should confirm specifics directly with Progressive before making financial decisions. Billmat LLC is not affiliated with Progressive Casualty Insurance Company.

You may not reproduce, republish, or redistribute content from this Site without permission, except for brief quotations with attribution and a link back to the original page.

Children’s privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact us about privacy

Questions about this policy or a request related to your personal data can be sent to hello@billmatguides.com or through our contact page.
